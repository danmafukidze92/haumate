Privacy notice

HaulMate collects your name, phone number, identity documents, a live selfie, and (for drivers) location while online or on a trip. We use them only to verify users, match loads with trucks, show shippers where their goods are, process payments and resolve disputes, in line with Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act.

Identity documents are stored encrypted and seen only by our verification team. Driver location is not tracked while offline. Phone numbers are not shared; calls and chat happen inside the app.

To access or delete your data, contact support through the app.
