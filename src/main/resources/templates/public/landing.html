How it works

Booked in minutes, delivered with proof

Sending goods

  1. Download the app and verify your ID with a quick selfie.
  2. Enter pickup, drop-off, weight and what you're moving.
  3. See the recommended price and set your offer.
  4. Pick a driver, pay securely, track the truck to your door.
  5. The receiver gives the driver a PIN. Payment is released.

Driving or running a fleet

  1. Register with your licence and truck papers.
  2. Go online to see loads near you, with the price up front.
  3. Take a load at the shipper's price or offer your own.
  4. Navigate, photograph the cargo, update each step.
  5. Deliver, enter the PIN, get paid to your wallet.
For drivers & fleet owners

Fill your truck, both ways

Stop waiting at depots and truck stops. Loads near you come to your phone, and you choose which ones are worth it.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How is the price worked out?

We recommend a price from road distance, truck size, current diesel price, tolls on the route and any extras like cold chain or urgency. You can offer more or less; drivers accept or counter.

When does the driver get paid?

You pay when you accept a driver. HaulMate holds the money and releases it to the driver only when the receiver gives them the delivery PIN. Cash on delivery is also available.

How do you verify drivers?

Every driver submits a national ID or passport, a live selfie check, their driver's licence and the truck's registration papers. Our team approves them before they can take loads.

What can I move?

Anything legal from 1 to 30+ tonnes: building materials, furniture, farm produce, retail stock, machinery, and more. Mark hazardous, fragile or refrigerated loads so the right truck comes.

Which phones are supported?

Android 7 and newer, and iPhone with iOS 15.5 and newer.
